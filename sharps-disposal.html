How to Safely Throw Away Needles at Home

Enchanted
MEDICAL AESTHETICS

Used needles can cause injuries and spread infections such as HIV and hepatitis B and C. Family members, housekeepers, sanitation workers, and children can all be hurt by a needle that is thrown away loosely. A few simple steps keep everyone safe.

The easiest way: use an Enchanted sharps container

  1. Pick up a small biohazard sharps container at our office.
  2. Drop each used syringe in right after your injection. Never recap.
  3. When it is about three-quarters full, snap the lid closed.
  4. Bring it back to us. Our biohazard service disposes of it at no charge.

How to avoid needle sticks

  • Don’t throw loose needles in the trash.
  • Don’t flush, bury, or burn needles.
  • Don’t use a container that needles can poke through.
  • Don’t use clear plastic or glass containers.
  • Don’t put needles in with your recycling.
  • Don’t leave needles where other people, children, or pets can reach them.

Other disposal options

County programs

Many Florida counties offer sharps drop-off or collection. Check your county’s website or call your county health department.

Mail-back services

Some companies sell a sharps container that you fill and mail back to them for disposal.

Household trash, only if allowed

If neither option is available, ask your garbage company whether sharps can go in household trash. If they can:

  1. Use a heavy-duty plastic bottle with a screw-on lid, such as a laundry detergent bottle.
  2. Stop filling it at about three-quarters full.
  3. Screw the lid on tight, seal it with heavy tape, and label it “Sharps: Do not recycle.”
  4. Place it in the middle of your trash, never in the recycling bin.
