Good to know

  • Tirzepatide works on two gut hormones, GIP and GLP-1. It quiets appetite and helps you stick with a reduced-calorie eating plan.
  • How well you tolerate it, and how much weight you lose, is different for everyone.
  • You can inject with or without food.
  • Keep exercising, eating well, and taking any vitamins or supplements you already use.
  • Need to change your injection day? Make sure at least 3 days have passed since your last dose.
  • Store the vial in the refrigerator. Do not freeze it.

See the Tirzepatide patient information page for side effects, safety, and storage.
