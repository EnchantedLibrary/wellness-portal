Your guide to self-injection

Subcutaneous Injections

Enchanted
MEDICAL AESTHETICS

A subcutaneous (SubQ) injection places medication into the layer of fat just under the skin, where it absorbs slowly and steadily. Use this guide alongside the training your Enchanted provider gave you, and only inject the medication, dose, and schedule on your prescription label.

1

Gather your supplies

Your medication vial (plus diluent if it comes as a powder), insulin syringes, alcohol pads, gauze or a cotton ball, a bandage, and a sharps container.

2

Read your insulin syringe

  • SubQ doses are measured with an insulin syringe marked in units.
  • On a U-100 syringe, 100 units = 1 mL, so 10 units = 0.1 mL.
  • Your label may list your dose in units or in mL. Confirm with your provider which mark to draw to before your first dose.
  • The needle comes attached and is thin and short, typically 29G–31G and 5/16" to ½" long. Use a new syringe for every injection.
3

Choose and prepare your site

  1. Wash your hands with soap and water for 20 seconds.
  2. Pick a site your provider approved (see below) and rotate sites each time.
  3. Clean the skin with an alcohol pad and let it air-dry.

Injection sites

Abdomen

Anywhere on the belly at least 2 inches from the belly button. The most common site.

Upper thigh

Front and outer area of the upper thigh, midway between hip and knee.

Upper arm

Fleshy back of the upper arm. Easiest with a trained partner.

Avoid scars, moles, stretch marks, bruises, and irritated skin.

4

Prepare your medication

  1. Let refrigerated medication come to room temperature. It stings less.
  2. Check the label for the right medication, the right dose, and the Beyond Use Date (BUD). Don’t use it if it’s past the BUD or looks different than expected.
  3. Powder vials: mix with the diluent exactly as your provider instructed.
5

Draw up your dose

  1. Remove the vial’s flip-off cap and wipe the rubber stopper with an alcohol pad.
  2. Push the needle straight into the stopper, then turn the vial upside down.
  3. Keep the needle tip in the liquid and slowly pull back a little past your dose.
  4. Tap out any air bubbles, then push the plunger to your exact prescribed mark.
  5. Check the dose against your label once more before injecting.
6

Give the injection

  1. Gently pinch up 1 to 2 inches of skin at your clean site.
  2. Insert the needle in one quick motion at 90°, or at 45° if your provider showed you.
  3. Release the pinch and push the plunger slowly until the syringe is empty.
  4. Count to 5, pull straight out, and press lightly with gauze. Don’t rub the site.
7

Clean up

  1. Put the syringe straight into your sharps container. Never recap.
  2. Store any remaining medication as the label directs.
  3. Wash your hands.

When to get help

Call 911
Trouble breathing, swelling of the face, lips, or throat, hives across the body, or fainting.
Call us
Redness, warmth, or swelling that spreads or worsens after 48 hours; drainage; fever; or severe pain.
What’s normal
Mild redness, slight stinging, or a small bump or bruise at the site for a day or two.
