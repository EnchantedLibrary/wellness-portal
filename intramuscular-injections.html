Your guide to self-injection

Intramuscular Injections

Enchanted
MEDICAL AESTHETICS

An intramuscular (IM) injection places medication deep into a large muscle, where it is absorbed steadily into the bloodstream. Use this guide alongside the training your Enchanted provider gave you, and only inject the medication, dose, and schedule on your prescription label.

1

Gather your supplies

Your medication vial (plus diluent if it comes as a powder), a syringe, a drawing needle and an injecting needle, alcohol pads, gauze or a cotton ball, a bandage, and a sharps container.

2

Know your syringe and needles

  • Syringe: 1 mL or 3 mL with a Luer-lock (screw-on) tip so the needle stays secure.
  • Gauge is needle thickness: the higher the number, the thinner the needle.
  • Drawing needle: thicker, 18G–21G, for pulling medication from the vial.
  • Injecting needle: thinner, 22G–25G, usually 1" to 1½" long to reach the muscle. Your provider chooses the length for your body and site.
  • Always switch to a fresh needle to inject. The rubber stopper dulls the tip.
3

Choose and prepare your site

  1. Wash your hands with soap and water for 20 seconds.
  2. Pick a site your provider approved (see below) and rotate sites each time.
  3. Clean the skin with an alcohol pad and let it air-dry.

Injection sites

Upper arm (deltoid)

Outer upper arm, 2–3 finger-widths below the shoulder bone. Small volumes only.

Outer thigh (vastus lateralis)

Middle third of the outer thigh. The easiest site to reach on your own.

Hip (ventrogluteal)

Side of the hip. Best done by a trained partner or at your visit.

Avoid scars, moles, bruises, and irritated skin.

4

Prepare your medication

  1. Let refrigerated medication come to room temperature. It stings less.
  2. Check the label for the right medication, the right dose, and the Beyond Use Date (BUD). Don’t use it if it’s past the BUD or looks different than expected.
  3. Powder vials: mix with the diluent exactly as your provider instructed.
5

Draw up your dose

  1. Remove the vial’s flip-off cap and wipe the rubber stopper with an alcohol pad.
  2. Push the drawing needle straight into the stopper, then turn the vial upside down.
  3. Keep the needle tip in the liquid and slowly pull back a little past your dose.
  4. Tap out any air bubbles, then push the plunger to your exact prescribed dose.
  5. Remove the drawing needle, twist on the injecting needle, and double-check the dose.
6

Give the injection

  1. Spread the skin taut with your free hand.
  2. Hold the syringe like a dart and insert the needle at 90° in one quick, smooth motion.
  3. Push the plunger slowly and steadily until the syringe is empty.
  4. Pull straight out and press gently with gauze. Massage the site only if your provider told you to.
7

Clean up

  1. Put the syringe and needle straight into your sharps container. Never recap.
  2. Store any remaining medication as the label directs.
  3. Wash your hands.

When to get help

Call 911
Trouble breathing, swelling of the face, lips, or throat, hives across the body, or fainting.
Call us
Redness, warmth, or swelling that spreads or worsens after 48 hours; drainage; fever; severe pain; or numbness or tingling.
What’s normal
Mild soreness, slight redness, or a small firm spot for a day or two. Oil-based medications can leave a lump for a few days.
